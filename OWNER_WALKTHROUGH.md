# Owner walkthrough — Loom script (~4 minutes)

Record this against the live site (or a Vercel Preview) for Prof. Nosike and his delegate. Speak plainly; he is the audience, not a developer.

---

**0:00 — Intro (15 s)**
"This is your new portfolio website. Everything you see is built from simple text files, so you can update it yourself — I'll show you how at the end."

**0:15 — Home (30 s)**
*Scroll the home page slowly.*
"The home page introduces you: your name and positioning, the key facts we agreed on, and shortcuts into your work. The map shows the countries you have worked in — hover a country to see your roles there."

**0:45 — Leadership and Education (30 s)**
*Open /leadership, scroll; open /education, scroll.*
"Leadership is your full career timeline, filterable by type — academic, visiting, and media or enterprise roles. Education lists your qualifications. If a date or title needs correcting, that's a one-line change in the content files."

**1:15 — Scholarship (45 s)**
*Open /scholarship; type a word in the search box; open one book card.*
"Scholarship is your publications explorer — books and articles, searchable and filterable by year, type and theme. Each entry has a copy-citation button so journalists and researchers can quote you correctly. Proceedings are on their own page with the confirmed ISBN details only."

**2:00 — Policy, Mentorship, Honours, Talks (45 s)**
*Quick clicks through /policy, /mentorship, /honours, /talks.*
"Policy shows your advisory roles; Mentorship your supervision record with the confirmed figures; Honours your awards — note we only publish items you have approved; Talks lists lectures with neutral descriptions."

**2:45 — Gallery, Events, About, Media kit, Contact (45 s)**
*Open /gallery and click a photo for the lightbox (ESC to close); /events timeline; /about; /media-kit; /contact.*
"Gallery is the approved photo selection with a keyboard-friendly lightbox. Events is the conference timeline. About has your bios and the world map. The media kit has your approved headshots and key facts for press. The contact form sends enquiries straight to your inbox — no email address is published anywhere."

**3:30 — How to edit (30 s)**
*Show EDIT_WORKFLOW.md or the GitHub repo.*
"To change anything — a new honour, a new publication, a corrected date — you edit a text file on GitHub and open a pull request. You get a preview link to check, and the site refuses to publish broken or private content automatically. The step-by-step guide is in the handover pack; we can do your first edit together on a call."

**3:55 — Close (15 s)**
"Questions? The full guide is in the repository README and CONTENT_GUIDE. Thank you."

---

**Recording tips:** use the production URL, 1080p, mic check first, pause between sections so YouTube chapters can be added later. Send the link to the client and keep a copy in the handover folder.
