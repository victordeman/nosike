Prof. Austin N. Nosike is a Nigerian economist and global management scholar who serves as Vice Chancellor/President of James Hope University in Lekki-Lagos and as President of the African Academy of Economics. He is a Professor of Economics, Business and Public Policy whose career spans more than three decades in higher education across Africa, Europe, the Americas and Asia.

He holds a B.Sc. (Hons) from the University of Nigeria, Nsukka, a Master's degree from the Universidad Católica San Antonio de Murcia in Spain, an MBA from the Universidad Azteca in Mexico, a PhD in Economics from the Universidad Central de Nicaragua, and a PhD in Business Administration from the Universidad Azteca. His postgraduate training includes fellowships at the Central European University in Budapest, the Institute of Social Studies in Warsaw and the International Institute of Human Rights in Strasbourg.

His academic career has taken him through leadership roles at universities in Nigeria, Rwanda, Uganda, Zambia, Burundi, the Dominican Republic, Italy, Spain and Hungary — as lecturer, research fellow, professor, dean, director of research, deputy vice chancellor and, since 2025, vice chancellor. He has also held visiting and concurrent appointments, including Visiting Professor at Kigali Independent University and at the Universidad de Granada.

Before academia, Prof. Nosike worked as a journalist — features writer at the Daily Star in Enugu, staff writer at The Statesman in Owerri and senior staff writer at News Flash — and later as a management consultant and publisher. That grounding shapes his ability to translate research into policy and public communication.

In public service and consultancy, he has advised the African Union Commission on human capacity building, consulted to the UN Economic Commission for Africa on water, sanitation and human ecology, served on Nigeria's National Universities Commission work as a resource person, and chaired the University Audit Panel of the National Council for Higher Education of Namibia in 2024. He has facilitated executive education for federal agencies through the Administrative Staff College of Nigeria.

A prolific scholar and editor, he has published more than 150 books, chapters, articles and papers, edited five international conference proceedings volumes, and holds editorial and board roles across African and international journals. He is founding President of the African Association of Journal Editors and sits on the governing board of the African Institute of Management, Technology and Development Studies in Kigali.

Prof. Nosike has supervised twelve PhD and thirty-eight Master's candidates to completion, with further candidates under current supervision, and serves as an external examiner for universities across the region. His distinctions include the 2025 University of Nigeria Alumni ICON Award, the International Youth of the Year Award (Africa), the 20th Century Award for Achievement in Education, and listings in Marquis Who's Who in the World and the Dictionary of International Biography. He speaks English and Spanish and has visited more than 30 countries.
