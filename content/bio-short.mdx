Prof. Austin N. Nosike is a Nigerian economist and global management scholar, Vice Chancellor/President of James Hope University, Lekki-Lagos, and President of the African Academy of Economics. With more than 30 years in higher education across Africa, Europe, the Americas and Asia, he has served as dean, director of research, deputy vice chancellor and vice chancellor, and as Professor of Economics, Business and Public Policy. He is a former journalist and management consultant, a past Chairperson of the University Audit Panel of Namibia's National Council for Higher Education, and a founder of the African Association of Journal Editors. He speaks English and Spanish.
